# Cylab Academy Write-ups

Walkthroughs of challenges from Cylab Academy and the picoCTF challenges
that sit under it. Each write-up focuses on the reasoning behind the
solve, not just the steps.

## Contents

| Section | What's inside | Status |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| [picoCTF](./picoctf/) | picoCTF challenges, grouped by category | In progress |
| other ctf  | Lessons and exercises | Planned |

## Scope

**Categories**
- Web
- Crypto
- Forensics
- Reverse Engineering
- Pwn

**What each write-up covers**
- Summary of the challenge and the core idea
- Recon and analysis, including dead ends
- Exploitation steps
- Flag (redacted as `CTF{...}`)
- Root cause and mitigation
- Takeaways, with a link to the matching note in `../../concepts/`

**What's not included**
- Live flags
- Solutions to active competitions
